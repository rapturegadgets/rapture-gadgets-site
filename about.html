Our story

A small shop with one rule

Never sell a phone we would not be happy to use ourselves. That single rule decides what we buy, what we test, and how we speak to a customer who knows nothing about phones.

Level 5, Shop 527Our display counter at Kooki Tower

Who we are

From a phone table
to a real counter

Rapture Gadgets started the way most Kampala phone businesses start — one person, one phone at a time, and a reputation that either grows or dies. Ours grew because we kept telling people the truth about the device in front of them.

Today we sit on the fifth floor of Kooki Tower with a display counter, an accessories wall, and a repair bench in the same room. We sell brand new sealed phones and neatly used UK imports, and we tell you plainly which is which.

  • Every used phone is graded and labelled before it goes on the shelf
  • The price on the wall is the price at the counter — no bargaining games
  • You test the phone with your own SIM before you pay

What we stand on

Four values we will not bend

01

Honesty first

If a phone has a fault, we say so before you pay. A customer who finds out later never comes back, and telling the truth costs us far less than losing them.

02

Test everything

Battery, cameras, Face ID, speakers, charging port and network. Checked on the counter, in front of you, with your own SIM card.

03

Fair prices

We publish our prices on the wall and on this website. We would rather earn a little less on each phone than argue about it all day.

04

Stay available

Questions after the sale are welcome. If something is confusing you about the phone you bought, come back or message us — that is part of the price.

Who we sell to

From one phone to twenty

Most days we sell a single phone to one person. But schools, offices and small companies also buy fleets from us, and they need different things — invoices, bank transfer, and phones that arrive on the same day.

Everyday customers

Someone replacing a broken phone, a parent buying a first smartphone for a secondary school student, or a customer outside Kampala ordering by WhatsApp and receiving the phone by courier. We explain every setting in plain language and we never rush you.

  • Free delivery inside Kampala on orders above UGX 1,000,000
  • Setup help: Google account, WhatsApp, transfers and screen guard

Business, school & office buyers

Companies need consistency: the same model, the same storage, delivered together, with paperwork that their accountant will accept. We keep a stock list for repeat buyers and can source specific models within a few days.

  • Bank transfer and a written quotation or receipt
  • Bulk pricing on five units or more — ask us for a quotation

Why we publish prices

Because guessing
is exhausting

Many phone shops in Kampala will not tell you a price until they have asked you five questions. We understand why — prices move. But we would rather publish the price, tell you it can change, and let you decide whether to come.

The boards in our shop and the tables on our Products page are written from the same list. If they ever differ, the board on the wall is the price we charge.

How the shop runs

A clear division of work is what keeps a small shop honest. Nobody handles the money and the stock alone.

StockChecks in new arrivals, tests every used phone and labels it.
