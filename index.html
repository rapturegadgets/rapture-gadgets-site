Before you buy

Questions we get every day

What does “UK Used” mean exactly?

It means the phone was previously owned and used in the United Kingdom, then imported here. Before we sell it we test the battery health, the cameras, Face ID or fingerprint, the speakers and the charging port. Anything that fails does not go on the shelf. We sell them in two honest grades, Neatly Used and Premium, and the grade is written on the price board.

Are the phones unlocked?

Yes. Every phone we sell is factory unlocked and works with MTN and Airtel SIM cards. If you want, we will put your SIM in at the counter and test it in front of you before you pay.

Can I see the phone before I pay?

Always. Come to Level 5, Shop 527 at Kooki Tower and we will hand you the unit. If you are outside Kampala, we can send a short video of the exact phone showing its IMEI and battery health before you send money.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes, we send phones by courier to every district. Delivery is by hand or by bus parcel, and you pay for the phone before it leaves the shop. Delivery inside Kampala is free on orders above UGX 1,000,000. Full details are on our Policies page.

Do you repair phones I did not buy from you?

Yes. Bring any iPhone, Samsung or Pixel and we will quote you before we open it. The quote does not change once we start, unless you ask us to add extra work.
