Service list

What we repair

We quote every job in person after looking at the phone. The price depends on the model and on which part is needed, so we never give a figure over the phone that we cannot keep.

Screen & touch replacement

Cracked glass, coloured lines, dead touch areas or a dark display. New screen fitted, sealed, and tested for Face ID and touch before you collect.

Battery replacement

Phone dying before lunch or shutting down at low battery? We fit a fresh cell and show you the new health reading on the screen before you leave.

Back glass replacement

A shattered back panel is both ugly and unsafe. We replace it carefully so the wireless charging and the camera lenses keep working properly.

Charging port repair

Phone not charging or the cable keeps falling out? We clean or replace the port and test it with both a cable and a wireless pad.

Water damage assessment

Dropped in a sink or caught in the rain? We open and dry the board first, then tell you honestly whether the phone is worth repairing.

Camera, speaker & mic

Blurry photographs, cracked lens glass, or people who cannot hear you on calls. Small parts, big difference, usually done the same day.

The process

Four steps, no surprises

01Bring the phone to Level 5, Shop 527, or send a photo on WhatsApp.
02We inspect it, find the fault and give you a written quote before opening it.
03You approve the quote. We do the work while you wait or call you when it is ready.
04You test the phone in front of us and pay only when you are satisfied.

Our repair promise

If the same fault comes back within 7 days of our repair, bring the phone back and we will look at it again at no charge. This covers our workmanship. It does not cover new physical damage, water damage after collection, or a fault in a part you asked us to supply yourself.
