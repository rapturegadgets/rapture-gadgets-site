Section two

Returns & warranty

We would rather give money back than keep a customer who is unhappy. These are the conditions, so there is no argument later.

What we will accept

  • A phone returned within 7 days, with the box, cable, adapter and any free screen guard.
  • A fault we did not disclose, found within 7 days — repaired free or replaced.
  • A repair where the same fault returns within 7 days.

What we will not accept

  • New scratches, cracked glass or water damage that happened after collection.
  • A phone opened or repaired by someone else.
  • A special order we brought in at your specific request.

How long a refund takes

Mobile money refunds are sent back to the number that paid and normally appear the same day. Bank transfer refunds are sent to the originating account and can take 3–5 working days to clear. Cash purchases are refunded in cash at the shop. Delivery charges are not refunded unless the error was ours.

Section three

Privacy notice

This notice explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how long we keep it. It follows Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

What we collect

Your name, your phone number, your delivery address, the phone you ordered, and the payment transaction ID. If you send us photographs of a phone for repair, we keep those too. We do not collect payment card numbers. We do not have the ability to take card payments on this website.

Why we collect it

To deliver the phone to you, to answer your questions, to keep a record of what we sold and repaired, and to meet the tax and record‑keeping duties that apply to a business in Uganda. That is all. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not use it for anything you did not ask for.

Who can see it

Only the staff of Rapture Gadgets who need it to serve you — the person selling, the person packing, and the person keeping accounts. Couriers receive only the name, phone number and address needed to complete your delivery. No one else sees your information.

How long we keep it

Order and repair records are kept for 7 years, because that is the period our accountants and the tax authorities expect a business to be able to look back over. WhatsApp chat is kept for as long as it sits on the phone. You may ask us to delete anything we hold about you by writing to eddiemund32@gmail.com.

Your rights under the 2019 Act

You have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have anything wrong corrected, and to ask us to delete it. If you believe we have mishandled your data, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office in Uganda. We will not make it difficult for you to exercise any of these rights.

Section four

Terms of sale

By buying from us, in the shop or through this website, you agree to the following.

1. Prices and grading

Prices are shown in Uganda shillings and can change without notice, because we buy at the prevailing exchange rate. The price board inside the shop is the final price. We sell in two grades: Brand New (sealed, in the retail box) and Neatly / Premium UK Used (previously owned, imported, tested). Every used phone is graded before it goes on sale and the grade is written on the price board.

2. Condition of a used phone

A used phone is not a new phone and we will never pretend otherwise. Minor cosmetic wear is normal and is not a fault. Battery health on a used phone is measured and told to you before you pay, and it will be below 100%. Please check the battery health, cameras, Face ID or fingerprint, speakers and charging port at the counter before you pay.

3. Payment and dispatch

We release a phone only after the money has actually appeared in our account, or on agreement of cash on delivery inside Kampala. We never accept a screenshot alone as proof of payment. See the How to Pay page for the full rule.

4. Warranty on a used phone

Every used phone carries a 7‑day functional warranty covering faults we did not disclose: for example a battery that drops charge far faster than stated, a camera that will not focus, or a charging port that does not work. It does not cover accidental damage, water, software changes you made yourself, or normal wear.

5. Repair work

We quote before we open a phone and the quote stands unless you ask for extra work. If the same fault returns within 7 days we look at it again at no charge. We are not responsible for a fault caused by a part you supplied yourself, or for data lost during a repair — please back up your phone before bringing it in.

6. Stolen or blocked devices

We do not buy or sell any phone that is blacklisted, blocked, still on a contract, or reported stolen. If a phone we sold is later found to be blacklisted, we will refund the purchase price in full. We record the IMEI of every used phone we buy.

7. Honest claims

We promise only what we can prove: that a phone is unlocked, that its battery health is as measured, and that its grade matches what is written on the board. We do not offer claims about awards, customer reviews or statistics that we cannot show you evidence for.

8. If someone impersonates us

Only the phone number printed on this website, +256 704 247 192, belongs to Rapture Gadgets. If anyone contacts you from another number claiming to be us, do not send money. Report it to us and to the police, and we will publish a warning on our pages and social accounts. We never post fake reviews about ourselves or about competitors, and we will act against anyone who posts fake reviews about us.
